# uat

`uat/characters/` holds the people Afterlife is built for: eight representative users at one European payments
company on GitLab, each with who they are, their jobs to be done, what "good" looks like to them and what makes them
stop trusting a screen. Priya is the operator, the person who adopts Afterlife on the group and decides which agent
may act alone; the others live around her decisions. The council's value member judges every feature against these
users. Every `.md` file in `uat/characters/` is read as a character, so nothing else goes in that folder. The cast was
adapted from Ascent's tracked characters, moved from GitHub to GitLab and rewritten around the Proof Block, earned
autonomy, the tripwire, writes on a click and the CRA clock.
